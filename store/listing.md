# Marketplace listing copy

## Application name

Lazybird: AI Voice Text to Speech & Read Aloud

## Short description

Read Google Docs, Slides and Sheets aloud in natural AI voices, and save the audio as MP3. 500+ voices in 150+ languages and accents. Free plan, no card needed.

## Detailed description

Lazybird reads your documents, presentations and spreadsheets aloud in natural AI voices. Pick a voice, choose what to read, and press Read aloud. Playback starts in seconds, even for long documents, and you can save the whole reading as one MP3 file.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Listen to a draft to catch typos, missing words and awkward sentences that your eyes skip over.
• Turn notes, articles and study material into audio you can follow along with or listen to later.
• Rehearse a talk: hear your slides or your speaker notes read aloud, slide by slide.
• Make a voiceover for a presentation or a video from your speaker notes, and save it as MP3.
• Hear lists, schedules and notes kept in a spreadsheet, one row at a time.
• Hear how a text sounds in another language or accent.

GOOGLE DOCS

• Read the selected text, everything from the cursor to the end, or the whole document.
• Lists and tables are read too, in reading order.

GOOGLE SLIDES

• Read this slide, every slide from this one on, or the whole presentation.
• Choose the slide text, the speaker notes, or both.
• Text on a slide is read top to bottom and left to right, and skipped slides are left out.
• The sidebar shows which slide is playing.

GOOGLE SHEETS

• Read the selected cells or the whole sheet, row by row.
• Each row is read on its own, and the sidebar shows which row is playing.

VOICES

• 500+ AI voices in 150+ languages and accents: narrators, conversational voices, characters and more.
• Search by name, style or accent, and filter by language and gender.
• Preview any voice before you pick it.
• Voices you clone in Lazybird show up under My voices, so you can read your documents in your own voice.
• Speed from 0.5× to 2×.

PLAYER

• Playback starts as soon as the first lines are ready and keeps going while the rest is generated.
• Pause, resume, stop and replay.
• Save as MP3: the whole reading as one file, named after your document.
• Your voice, speed and reading choices are remembered for next time.

GETTING STARTED

1. Install Lazybird.
2. In Docs, Slides or Sheets, open Extensions > Lazybird > Read aloud.
3. Enter your email and type the 6-digit code we send you. If you're new to Lazybird, this creates a free account. If you already have a Lazybird API key, you can paste it instead.
4. Pick a voice and a speed, choose what to read, and press Read aloud.

PLANS

The free plan includes 10,000 characters a month, with no card needed. Each character read uses one credit. If you need more, paid plans are available at lazybird.app/pricing.

PRIVACY

• Lazybird uses only the "current file" permissions: it can read the document, presentation or spreadsheet you open it in, and nothing else in your Drive.
• It reads only the part you choose, only when you press Read aloud, and never changes your files.
• The text you choose is sent to Lazybird's servers to generate the audio. Lazybird does not sell your data and does not use your text to train AI models.
• The add-on has no ads and no tracking.

The add-on is open source under the MIT license: github.com/lazybirdai/google-workspace-addon-lazybird

MORE FROM LAZYBIRD

Lazybird Studio at lazybird.app is a full voiceover editor for videos, podcasts, audiobooks and courses: long scripts, dialogues with more than one voice, and voice cloning. The same voices are available in the add-on.
