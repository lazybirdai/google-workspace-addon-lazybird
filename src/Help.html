Lazybird reads your Docs, Sheets and Slides aloud with natural AI voices. Open it from Extensions → Lazybird → Read aloud.

Getting started

  • Enter your email in the sidebar and type the 6-digit code we send you. If you're new to Lazybird, this creates a free account.
  • Or paste an API key from Lazybird Studio settings.
  • Pick a voice and a speed, choose what to read, and press Read aloud.

What it reads

  • Docs: the selection, from the cursor to the end, or the whole document.
  • Slides: this slide, from this slide on, or all slides; slide text, speaker notes or both.
  • Sheets: the selected cells or the whole sheet.

Slide voiceovers

In Slides, Add voiceover puts the reading of each slide on that slide as a small clip that plays by itself when you present. The clips are saved in a Lazybird voiceovers folder in your Drive. The first time, select the presentation in the window that opens so Lazybird can edit it.

Credits

Each character read uses one credit from your Lazybird plan. The free plan includes 10,000 characters a month. See plans.
